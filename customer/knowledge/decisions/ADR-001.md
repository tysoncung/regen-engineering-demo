## Decision

Deleting a customer marks the record deleted and retains it. It does not remove
the record.

## Why

Orders reference the customer and must survive customer deletion for the legal
retention period. A hard delete would either orphan orders or cascade into
destroying financial records.

## Alternatives considered

- **Hard delete with anonymisation.** Rejected: irreversible, and an
  anonymisation bug cannot be recovered from.
- **Hard delete with an archival copy.** Rejected: two sources of truth for the
  same customer, which is exactly the drift this methodology exists to avoid.

## Consequences

- Emails are never released for reuse ([BR-001](../rules/BR-001.md)).
- Authentication must explicitly reject deleted accounts ([BR-003](../rules/BR-003.md)).
- Purging at the end of the retention period is out of scope for this module.
