---
id: BR-001
type: business-rule
title: Customer email must be unique
status: active
since: 2026-07-30
affects: [customer]
implemented_by: [customer]
verified_by: [CT-001]
---

An email address identifies at most one customer account.

Comparison is case-insensitive and ignores surrounding whitespace, so
`  ADA@Example.com  ` and `ada@example.com` are the same address.

Registration with an email already in use is rejected with HTTP 409. The
response body must not reveal whether an account exists, so it carries a
generic message rather than "email already registered".

A deleted customer keeps their email, so it is never released for reuse. See
[ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001.md).

The stored form of the email is deliberately unspecified: no endpoint returns
it, so whether it is kept verbatim or normalised is unobservable. If an
endpoint ever exposes it, that choice must be specified first.
