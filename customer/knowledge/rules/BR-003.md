title: Deleted customers cannot authenticate
status: active

affects: [customer]

A customer whose account is deleted cannot authenticate.

Deletion is soft: the record is retained, along with any orders that reference
it. See [ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001.md).

Authentication against a deleted account fails with HTTP 401 carrying exactly
the same generic message as an unknown email, so account existence is not
disclosed. The two cases must be indistinguishable to a caller.

Authentication takes an email only. There are no passwords in this system, so
the deleted case and the unknown case are the only two failures that exist.

Deletion restricts authentication and nothing else. A deleted customer's
addresses and orders remain readable, because orders must survive deletion
(see [ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001.md)).
