---
id: BR-003
type: business-rule
title: Deleted customers cannot authenticate
status: active
since: 2026-07-30
affects: [customer]
implemented_by: [customer]
verified_by: [CT-003]
---

A customer whose account is deleted cannot authenticate.

Deletion is soft: the record is retained, along with any orders that reference
it. See [ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001.md).

Authentication against a deleted account fails with HTTP 401 and the same
generic message as a wrong password, so account existence is not disclosed.
