title: Email uniqueness on registration
status: active

## Scenario: an unused email registers successfully

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
Then registration succeeds

## Scenario: a duplicate email is rejected

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
When someone registers with "ada@example.com"
Then registration is rejected
And the response does not disclose that an account exists

## Scenario: uniqueness ignores case and surrounding whitespace

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
When someone registers with "  ADA@Example.com  "
Then registration is rejected

## Scenario: a deleted customer's email stays taken

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
And the customer's account is deleted
When someone registers with "ada@example.com"
Then registration is rejected
