## Scenario: an active customer authenticates

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
When the customer authenticates with "ada@example.com"
Then authentication succeeds

## Scenario: a deleted customer cannot authenticate

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
And the customer's account is deleted
When the customer authenticates with "ada@example.com"
Then authentication fails

## Scenario: an unknown email fails the same way

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
When the customer authenticates with "nobody@example.com"
Then authentication fails
