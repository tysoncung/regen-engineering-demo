## Scenario: the twentieth address is accepted

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
And the customer already has 19 addresses
When the customer adds address "20 Twentieth St"
Then the address is added
And the customer has 20 addresses

## Scenario: the twenty-first address is rejected

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
And the customer already has 20 addresses
When the customer adds address "21 Twenty-first St"
Then adding the address is rejected
And the rejection message is generic
And the customer has 20 addresses

## Scenario: a rejected add leaves the default alone

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
And the customer has addresses "1 First St"
And the customer already has 20 addresses
When the customer adds address "21 Twenty-first St"
Then adding the address is rejected
And the default address is "1 First St"
And the customer has 20 addresses

## Scenario: removing an address frees a slot

Given a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
And the customer has addresses "1 First St"
And the customer already has 20 addresses
When the customer deletes address "1 First St"
And the customer adds address "21 Twenty-first St"
Then the address is added
And the customer has 20 addresses

## Scenario: the limit is per customer

Given a customer registered with "grace@example.com"
And the customer already has 20 addresses
And a customer registered with "ada@example.com"
When the customer adds address "1 First St"
Then the address is added
And the customer has 1 address
