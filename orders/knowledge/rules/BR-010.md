---
id: BR-010
type: business-rule
title: An order captures its shipping address rather than referencing it
status: active
since: 2026-07-30
affects: [orders, customer]
implemented_by: [orders]
verified_by: [CT-010]
---

An order cannot be placed unless the customer has at least one address. An
attempt without one is rejected with HTTP 400.

The order ships to the customer's default address at the moment of placement,
unless the customer chooses a different one of their own addresses at checkout.
Choosing an address that does not belong to the customer is rejected with
HTTP 400.

Either way the order stores a **copy** of the address rather than a reference
to it.

The copy is the point. A customer who later edits or deletes that address must
not retroactively change where a past order was sent.

Placing an order marks the address used, which feeds the promotion rule in
[BR-002](../../../customer/knowledge/rules/BR-002.md).
